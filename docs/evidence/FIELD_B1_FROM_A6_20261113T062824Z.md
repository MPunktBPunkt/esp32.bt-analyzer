Reading:

- Six SPP-like servers. Channel 5 carries a vendor 128-bit UUID (`deca-fade`), channels 6–10 plain SPP `0x1101`.
- An Android client that resolves "SPP `0x1101`" (BclCarLink `createRfcommSocketToServiceRecord(00001101-…)`)
  lands on the **first matching record**, i.e. channel 6 — not on channel 5.
- Channel 5 here is the HU's `deca-fade` server. It is **not** the lab dummy HU channel 5; do not conflate.
- No AVRCP Target record: the HU is AVRCP CT only. MAP-MNS means the HU is a MAP client (MCE).
- AVRCP-CT features `0x004d` **ohne** Browsing-Bit — trotzdem nutzt die HU PSM `0x1B` (GetFolderItems). Record-Name „A2DP SNK“ ist ALPS-Labeling.
