## What happens (MEASURED)

Same sequence in all four sessions (ours to HU channel 2, the HU's three to our channel 13):

| # | HU (HF) | Our AG |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `AT+BRSF=255` | `+BRSF: 3680`, `OK` |
| 2 | `AT+BAC=1,2` | `OK` |
| 3 | `AT+CIND=?` | `+CIND: ("service",(0-1)),("call",(0-1)),("callsetup",(0-3)),("callheld",(0-2)),("signal",(0-5)),("roam",(0-1)),("battchg",(0-5))`, `OK` |
| 4 | `AT+CIND?` | `+CIND: 0,0,0,0,0,0,0`, `OK` |
| 5 | `AT+CMER=3,0,0,1` | `OK` |
| 6 | – | `+BCS: 2` (codec connection, mSBC) |
| 7 | `AT+CHLD=?` | **`ERROR`** |
| 8 | DISC on the DLCI | – |

Feature bits:

- HU `AT+BRSF=255` = `0xFF`: EC/NR, **three-way calling**, CLI, voice recognition, remote volume,
  enhanced call status, enhanced call control, codec negotiation.
- Our `+BRSF: 3680` = `0xE60`: reject call, enhanced call status, codec negotiation, HF indicators, eSCO S4.
  **No three-way calling bit.**
- Our SDP: HFP AG **1.9**, SupportedFeatures `0x0120` (wideband + super wideband speech), RFCOMM 13.

## Who is wrong

- By HFP, `AT+CHLD=?` belongs to SLC setup only if **both** sides support three-way calling. Our AG
  does not announce it, so after `AT+CMER` the SLC is complete and the AG may start codec negotiation (`+BCS: 2`).
- The HU sends `AT+CHLD=?` anyway, ignores `+BCS`, and tears the link down on `ERROR`.
  Every real phone announces three-way calling, so the HU path "AG without three-way" is probably never exercised.
- Result: HU-side deviation, triggered by an AG that has no telephony behind it.
